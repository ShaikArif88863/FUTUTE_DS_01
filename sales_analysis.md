
Business Sales Performance Analytics

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand business performance, identify profitable product categories, compare regional sales, and discover trends over time.

The goal is to provide useful insights and recommendations that can help a business improve sales and profitability.

2. Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Analyze sales and profit by region.
- Study monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

3. Dataset Information

- Dataset: Superstore Sales Dataset
- Format: CSV
- Source: "Kaggle Superstore Dataset" (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
- Data includes: Order dates, product names, categories, sales, profit, discounts, and regions.

4. Tools and Technologies

- GitHub for project storage and documentation.
- CSV for storing sales data.
- Excel or Google Sheets for data cleaning and analysis.
- Charts and visualizations for presenting findings.

5. Data Cleaning

The dataset will be checked for:

- Missing values.
- Duplicate records.
- Incorrect data formats.
- Consistent date, sales, and profit values.

Any required corrections will be documented before analysis.

6. Sales Analysis

The analysis will answer the following questions:

1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. Which products generate the highest sales?
3. Which product categories perform best?
4. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
5. How do sales change over time?
